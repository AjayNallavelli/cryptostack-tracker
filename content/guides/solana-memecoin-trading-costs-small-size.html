At small size, what a memecoin trade costs depends less on the headline fee and more on the fixed costs around it. This guide lists what each common Solana app or bot charges to buy and sell a pump.fun coin on its bonding curve, cheapest first, then works out a full buy-and-sell (round trip) at 0.04 SOL. Fees change often; every number links to its source, checked Oct 23, 2026.

The fee nobody avoids: pump.fun's curve

  • Every buy and every sell on a pump.fun bonding curve pays 1.25% (0.30% to the creator, 0.95% to the protocol), whichever app or bot sends the trade. That is about 2.5% per round trip, so every trade starts about 2.5% down.
  • Trading on the pump.fun website or mobile app adds a 0.45% platform fee (0.40% with a referral) since Oct 17, 2026. It does not apply when you trade the same coin through another app.
  • Buying a new token opens a token account that holds about 0.002 SOL. That is a deposit, not a fee: you get it back by closing the empty account after you sell.

The paper scoreboard scores every call with an estimated 2.5% round-trip cost. As the table shows, the real cost at 0.04 SOL is at least that, and can be several times higher with default settings. All figures are machine-readable in trading_costs.json.

A note on BullX

BullX suspended trading, including memecoin trading, in its current app from June 1, 2026. Wallet functions and withdrawals stayed open.

Educational, not financial advice. Fees are copied from each provider's own pages on the date checked and can change at any time. Small trades in new tokens can lose most of their value on costs and price moves alone.
