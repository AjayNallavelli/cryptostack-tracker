Indian tax notes (official sources, not advice)

These are the main central rules that official Income-tax materials describe for VDAs. Thresholds, forms and your own facts can change; read the Act and, if needed, a qualified tax professional.

  • Section 115BBH of the Income-tax Act, 1961 (in force from assessment year 2023–24) taxes income from the transfer of a VDA at 30%. Official text says no deduction is allowed except the cost of acquisition, and a loss from a VDA transfer cannot be set off against other income or carried forward.
  • Section 194S requires the payer of consideration for transfer of a VDA to a resident to deduct 1% TDS, with effect from 1 July 2022. CBDT Circular No. 14 of 2022 restates the statutory de minimis: no deduction if the consideration paid by a “specified person” does not exceed ₹50,000 in the financial year, or ₹10,000 for any other payer. On an exchange, the exchange typically deducts; in a peer-to-peer transfer, the buyer is generally the deductor. Circular No. 13 of 2022 is the guideline for transactions on or through an exchange. TDS is an advance of tax, not the final 30% liability.
  • Exchange help pages (ZebPay’s pricing page; CoinSwitch’s TDS FAQ) also describe 1% TDS and say the amount can be claimed when filing a return. Those pages are secondary to the Act and CBDT circulars.

Sources: Section 115BBH (Income Tax Department); CBDT Circular No. 13 of 2022 (22 June 2022) and Circular No. 14 of 2022 (28 June 2022).

How to read this

  • A lower published international fee does not by itself make a venue “better” if you need a documented INR rail, or if that venue is not offered to you.
  • 1% TDS is not the same as the 30% tax on VDA income under 115BBH. TDS is withheld up front; the 30% is computed on the return, with official limits on deductions and loss set-off.
  • GST on exchange fees, where a venue charges it, is separate from TDS.
  • Use the trading fee calculator to apply a published percentage to a size you type. For risk maths, see trading techniques.

FAQ

Is crypto legal in India?

This page does not give a legal opinion. The Income-tax Act taxes transfers of virtual digital assets, and exchanges describe FIU-IND registration and KYC under PMLA. That is not the same as a product-by-product licence, and it is not advice to use any venue.

Why are CoinDCX and CoinSwitch fees left blank?

Because a complete official maker/taker table could not be verified from the pages we were able to read on 11 Nov 2026. The site’s rule is to leave unverified fees blank rather than copy them from reviews.

Does the Binance referral apply in India?

The button is a disclosed referral link. CryptoStack may earn a commission if you use it. Whether Binance offers a given product or INR rail to you is a question for Binance’s current terms, not for this page.
