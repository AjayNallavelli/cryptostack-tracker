This page compares published fees and documented features for widely used Solana trading terminals and Telegram bots, using this site’s tool pages. Fee figures in the table were last read from each vendor’s official docs on the date in that row (mostly 22–23 Oct 2026). They were not all re-opened on 11 Nov 2026. A listing is not a ranking of “the best bot”, and it is not a recommendation to trade memecoins or to sign up.

Best for, in one line

  • Best for the lowest published platform commission: Jupiter Manual mode, because official docs state a 0% Jupiter commission (you still set priority fees and tips yourself; there is no MEV protection in Manual mode).
  • Best for a documented volume-tier net fee: Axiom, because official docs publish a net fee from 0.95% (Wood) down to 0.75% (Champion) via SOL cashback, and a 10% trading-fee discount for referred sign-ups.
  • Best for a simple published flat 1% web terminal: Photon, because its fee page states 1% of the SOL amount on every buy and every sell, with no published volume discount.
  • Best for a Telegram bot with a published 1% successful-swap fee: BONKbot or Trojan, because both official fee pages state 1% per successful trade or swap. Reward or cashback percentages are not fully published on those pages.
  • Best for combining a scanner with 1% in-app trading across several chains: GMGN, because official docs state a 1% handling fee when you trade through it, and list Solana plus other networks.

BullX Neo published a 1% platform fee, but it suspended trading in its current app from 1 June 2026, so it is not a current trading option on those terms. Maestro publishes 1% per successful buy and sell on several chains, including Solana.

What the fee does not include

  • At small size, Axiom’s documented default priority fee and tip (0.001 SOL each) can cost more than the 1% venue fee. That is why a 0.04 SOL round trip at those defaults was about 14.5% in the costs guide, versus about 3% for Jupiter Manual with a 0.0001 SOL priority fee.

FAQ

Which bot is cheapest?

There is no single answer. The lowest published platform commission in this set is Jupiter Manual at 0%, but you still pay network fees and any launchpad fee. A 1% terminal plus high default tips can be several times more expensive at small size than a 0% or 0.85% route with lower fixed fees. Work a size through the fee calculator.

Are these all of the Solana trading bots?

No. This list is the terminals and bots this site has already checked against official fee pages. Absence is not a judgment.

Is this a recommendation to use Axiom’s referral?

No. The Axiom button is a disclosed referral link. Axiom’s docs say referred sign-ups receive a trading-fee discount; CryptoStack may earn a commission if you use the link. It is not a reason to sign up or to trade.
