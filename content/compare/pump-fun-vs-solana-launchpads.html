This page compares published launch and trading fees for three Solana launchpads that document a fee schedule: pump.fun, Raydium LaunchLab, and Moonshot (the official FAQ now says the product is called Moonit). A fourth venue, LetsBONK, is included only to say that an official fee table was not found. This is not a recommendation to launch or trade a token, and it is not a pick of any coin.

Best for, in one line

  • Best for a published “no platform fee” coin create, plus a documented 1.25% bonding-curve trade fee: pump.fun, because its fees page (updated 17 Oct 2026) lists no platform fee to create a coin and a 1.25% curve fee (0.30% creator / 0.95% protocol), with a 0.015 SOL graduation fee.
  • Best for a lower published pre-graduation swap fee (1%) and a 0.25% post-graduation CPMM fee: Raydium LaunchLab, because Raydium’s official fee-comparison page lists a 1% default on LaunchLab bonding-curve trades, about 0.04 SOL graduation paid from curve reserves, and 0.25% on the post-graduation CPMM pool.
  • Best for a published flat 1% trade fee, a 2 SOL migration fee, and a stated 432 SOL graduation market cap with a choice of Meteora, Raydium or Uniswap: Moonshot / Moonit, because that is what the official FAQ states.

How to use this table

  • A third-party terminal fee (often 1%; see Solana trading bots) is added on top of the launchpad fee when you do not trade through the launchpad’s own app.
  • On pump.fun, the 0.45% app platform fee applies only to the official web or mobile app, not to Terminal or third-party interfaces.
  • Work a size through the trading fee calculator (the “pump.fun app + curve” preset uses 0.45% + 1.25%). The bonding-curve guide explains pump.fun’s curve in more detail.
  • New launches are high risk. See the memecoin risk checklist and timing new token launches.

FAQ

Why is LetsBONK in the table if the fees are blank?

It is a Solana launchpad people compare with pump.fun, so omitting it would be less honest than showing it with an unverified label. Its official terms say fees may apply and should appear before you confirm. Third-party sites quote percentages; those are not used here because they are not official.

Is Moonshot the same as Moonit?

The official FAQ we read is still hosted at docs.moonshot.cc and opens with “Moonshot is now Moonit”. The fee figures in the table are taken from that FAQ. Check the live docs if the product name or fee line has moved.

Does a lower curve fee make a launch safer?

No. A fee is a cost, not a safety check. Graduation rules, liquidity locks and audits (Moonshot’s FAQ, for example, links an Ackee audit) still do not make a token safe to buy.
