Best for, in one line

  • Best for the lowest published regular-user spot maker fee among these three: OKX, because its September 2026 fee-adjustment notice lists Regular spot at 0.0800% maker / 0.1000% taker.
  • Best for a published token discount on regular spot rates: Binance, because the official spot schedule lists 0.100% / 0.100% for regular users and 0.07500% / 0.07500% with a 25% BNB discount.
  • Best for the lowest published regular-user perp taker fee among these three: Binance and OKX, which both publish a 0.0500% taker rate on the standard USDT-margined (or FAQ example) schedule, versus Bybit VIP 0 at 0.0550% taker. Notes that did not go into the table

  • Binance’s USDⓈ-M fee page can also describe promotions (for example a 0 maker-fee period). This table uses the standard Regular User column, not a promotion that may have ended.
  • OKX’s FAQ states that rates shown when you are logged out can differ from the rates on your account. The 0.02% / 0.05% futures figures are the rates used in that FAQ’s worked examples, not a substitute for the live schedule at okx.com/fees.
  • Bybit’s help article, last updated 5 Nov 2026, also publishes separate, higher schedules for fiat-crypto spot, an “Adventure Zone”, pre-market perps and an Innovation Zone. Those are not in the headline row.
  • Funding, liquidation fees, withdrawal fees and spread are separate from maker/taker rates. The trading fee calculator can apply these percentages to a size you type.
  • Access, fiat rails and tax rules vary by country. For INR on-ramps and Indian TDS notes, see crypto exchanges for Indian users.

FAQ

Is OKX always cheaper than Binance on spot?

Not necessarily. At the published regular-user rates, OKX’s 0.08% maker is lower than Binance’s 0.10% maker, while both list a 0.10% taker. With Binance’s published 25% BNB discount, Binance regular spot is 0.075% / 0.075%, which is lower than OKX regular. Your logged-in tier may be different.

Do these rates include funding?

No. Funding is a periodic payment between longs and shorts on perpetual contracts, not a maker/taker trading fee. See funding-rate carry.

Why is there a Binance referral button?

It is a disclosed referral link stored in this site’s referrals file. CryptoStack may earn a commission if you use it. It is not a recommendation to use Binance. Bybit and OKX are linked to their official sites until a referral URL is added for them.
