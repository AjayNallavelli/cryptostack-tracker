How to read this comparison

This page is education only. It is not financial advice, not a ranking of chains to buy, and not a suggestion to trade any style. When this page says a style is a better fit or best suited, it means “best suited because of documented fees, block or slot times, liquidity venues, launchpads, perps venues, and MEV or sandwich characteristics” — not “you should do this.” A style can be a mechanical fit and still be a bad idea for a given person, or unprofitable.

Every fee, block-time and venue claim below is taken from official or public docs already cited on the chain explainers, plus a dated public volume snapshot from DefiLlama. Re-check those sources; figures change. There is no dedicated liquidity-providing (LP) technique page on this site yet; LP is listed in the table where it comes up, with the usual impermanent-loss and smart-contract caveats. Airdrop or points farming is listed only as a style people use when a project has actually published a program. Nothing here forecasts that any chain will run one.

Related method pages: timing new token launches, memecoin risk checklist, DCA, grid trading, funding-rate carry, spot–futures basis, avoiding liquidation, TWAP, position sizing.

DEX volume snapshot

On 11 Nov 2026 (Asia/Calcutta), a public pull of DefiLlama’s DEX overview (defillama.com/dexs/chains, via the public api.llama.fi/overview/dexs breakdown) showed approximately the following 24-hour spot DEX volume, rounded: Ethereum $1.99B, Solana $1.56B, Base $1.11B, BNB Chain (BSC) $977M, Robinhood Chain $573M, Hyperliquid L1 $386M, Arbitrum $210M, Avalanche $89M, TRON $68M, Monad $37M, X Layer $28M, Arc $16M, MegaETH $1.4M. Stable printed a negligible figure (tens of dollars) in that breakdown. Blast did not appear among the large venues in that 24-hour breakdown. These are one-day snapshot figures, not a quality ranking, and they are not perps volume. DefiLlama’s paid derivatives API was not used here. Hyperliquid’s distinctive venue is a native perpetual order book (HyperCore), which official docs describe separately from HyperEVM spot activity — so the $386M DEX figure understates why perps styles are listed for that stack.

Solana

Styles that are best suited to Solana, because of official fees, 200 ms slots and the Pump.fun / Raydium / Jupiter venue set, plus high DEX volume in the 11 Nov 2026 snapshot: short-horizon launch timing and DEX activity, and ordinary DEX swings in more liquid Solana markets. CEX DCA and grid remain a fit for majors, but they do not use the chain’s slot speed. Main risks are rugs, bundles, priority fees and attention dumps. Read the Solana explainer, the memecoin checklist and launch timing.

BNB Chain

Styles that are best suited to BNB Chain, because of official ~0.45s blocks, typically low BNB fees, PancakeSwap and Four.meme, and large BSC DEX volume in the snapshot: launchpad-style activity and DEX swings, plus CEX DCA, grid, funding and basis next to Binance (Referral link). That adjacency is a venue fact, not a recommendation to open an account. Main risks are launch rugs and the usual gap between CEX and on-chain execution. Read the BNB Chain explainer.

Ethereum

Styles that are best suited to Ethereum L1, because it led this snapshot in 24h DEX volume and has deep Uniswap-style venues: DEX swings and, where the book is deep enough, TWAP of larger size. Micro memecoin sniping is a poorer fit because of ~12s slots, variable L1 gas, and documented MEV/sandwich risk. Funding, basis and small-size DCA often happen on a CEX or on a cheaper L2 rather than on L1, which is a cost fact, not a product pitch. Read the Ethereum explainer and Ethereum’s MEV documentation.

HyperEVM

Styles that are best suited to this stack, because official docs put the perpetual order book, funding and liquidations on HyperCore next to HyperEVM: funding-rate carry, basis-style hedges, and other perps methods, always with liquidation risk in view. The EVM itself is still described as alpha; launch culture is thinner than Solana or BNB Chain. Confusing a HyperCore perp with a HyperEVM token is a common operational failure. Read the HyperEVM explainer and, for the related name, What is Elysium?.

Base

Styles that are best suited to Base, because it is a 200 ms OP Stack L2 with ETH gas and, in the 11 Nov 2026 snapshot, the third-largest 24h DEX volume in this set: lower-fee DEX swings versus Ethereum L1, and some short-horizon token activity in pools that actually have liquidity. L2 withdrawal delays and sequencer risk remain. There is no Coinbase referral on this site. Read the Base explainer.

The other chains on this site

Arbitrum One is a fit for cheaper-than-L1 DEX swings, with an official ~6.4-day dispute window. Avalanche C-Chain supports ordinary DEX and CEX-adjacent styles, not a primary memecoin-launch culture. TRON styles follow SunSwap/SunPump and CEX activity, with Bandwidth/Energy fee mechanics. Monad is fast on paper and thinner in the volume snapshot, so caution and paper-style sizing come first. Robinhood Chain had high DefiLlama DEX volume on the date checked, but officially it is an Orbit L2 with a Uniswap listing and ~7-day withdrawals — treat the volume figure as a snapshot, not as a reason to snipe memecoins. X Layer is OKX-adjacent with modest volume (no OKX referral here). Blast and MegaETH are thinner or newer; MegaETH’s official latency is notable, its 24h DEX volume in the snapshot was not. Arc and Stable are payments-oriented (USDC gas and USDT0 gas); they are not framed here as memecoin-launch venues, and Stable’s DEX volume on the date checked was negligible.

Points or airdrop farming is only a “style” when a project has published a program with written rules. This page does not forecast one.

Risks

  • A mechanical fit is not an edge. Fees, speed and volume do not make a trade profitable.
  • Volume snapshots change daily and can be dominated by a few pools or by wash-prone flow. DefiLlama is a public aggregator, not an official chain document.
  • MEV, sandwiches, rugs, liquidation, bridging and custody risk apply even on a “well suited” chain.
  • This page is education only. It is not a recommendation to use any chain, venue or style, and not a suggestion to buy any token.
