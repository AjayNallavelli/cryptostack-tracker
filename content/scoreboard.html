Notes

  • Every entry is a paper call, not advice. No coin here is a recommendation, and past paper results say nothing about future ones.
  • Logged entry price = market cap when the call was named ÷ 1 billion supply. For Arnaud, FORM and Pumuckel that price was 16 to 20%+ above anything that traded, so from Oct 23, 2026 new calls use the first real trade after the call, logged to the second. The logged results above are left as they were.
  • Exits follow the written exit line: -30% stop, or half at 2x with the rest on the same stop. The logged stops on calls 2 and 5-9 are paper fills at exactly -30%; ship's is the real fill after it gapped through its stop.
  • Stops fail on gaps. In the exit study every stop filled below its level, and single sells sent php, Pumuckel and ship straight through. Size as if a failed stop costs 50 to 70%, not 30%.
  • ship closed as a win at +23.7% net: half was sold at 2x, it peaked at +305%, then one sell gapped the rest from about $82k to $17.9k.
  • php and YAI were named before the exit rules existed and were marked to the floor. Re-reading their full trade history: php briefly touched 2x (about 9 minutes after the call) before collapsing, and YAI would have hit its stop about 2 minutes after the call. The logged results stand.
  • The 2.5% round-trip cost is an estimate, applied equally to every call. At 0.04 SOL, real round trips run from about 3.0% to 15% depending on the app and its priority-fee settings; see trading costs at small size.
  • 10 calls is a tiny sample. What these calls do and don't show is written up in the first 10 paper calls and the exit study.
